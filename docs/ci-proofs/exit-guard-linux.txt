The cause was not a bash 3.2 versus bash 5 trap difference. env.sh activated conda
BEFORE the caller could install the exit guard, the test signalled on a fixed 0.4s
delay, and that activation costs 4.1s on this node and 0s on a Mac with no miniconda at
all. The signal therefore landed with no trap installed, and bash died of the raw
SIGTERM. See the DECISION-LOG entry of 2026-09-07 for the fix.
