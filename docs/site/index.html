Bayesian causal faithfulness · phase 2 running

Does the reasoning cause the answer, or just accompany it?

Frontier safety leans on reading a model's chain of thought. This project treats that chain as a mediator in a causal graph, measures how much of the answer actually travels through it, and turns the one assumption nobody can verify into a dial you can turn.

The short version

Six numbers: what works, what was found, and what is still open.

01 / RECOVERY 0.21

On synthetic data with hidden confounding built in, an analyst who assumes there is none reads a faithful path of 0.43 when the truth is 0.21. The sensitivity sweep recovers 0.21 at the true confounding level and reports how much confounding the verdict can absorb.

02 / SCALE 72 of 216

Nine-arm cells banked across six models, three substrates and four cue families. Two roster subjects are held by ruling: one cannot be served under the pinned mode, one has a tokenizer that does not round-trip.

03 / MEASUREMENT 30 of 30

Completions that stay identical at temperature 0 under batch-invariant serving, at every load measured. Without it only 11 of 30 match once eight requests share a batch, and letter logprobs move by up to 0.875 nats. Every powered run re-proves determinism on its own server.

04 / JUDGES 10 of 10

Pass thresholds that one judge configuration clears, all written down before the first judge ran: Qwen3-32B on the revised question prompt. No majority panel clears them in any composition, so the panel stays unfrozen and the human calibration labels stay sealed.

05 / THE FINDING 0.146 vs 0.025

A randomized anchor says a lot of the hint effect travels with the chain. The mediation fit says almost none, with an interval covering zero. They disagree on every model. That disagreement is the most interesting result here.

06 / SELF-CRITIQUE 0.8428

The largest in an eleven-family synthetic battery, sitting on a world whose true mediated effect is zero. A zero-mediation world and a fully mediated one produce the same report. So rho* cannot rank anything.

01

01 · the problem

Safety cases are being written on reasoning that might be decoration.

If a model's stated reasoning does not cause its answer, three things that the field depends on quietly stop working.

Oversight

Scalable oversight breaks

Watching the chain of thought is the cheapest oversight signal available. If the chain is written alongside the answer rather than producing it, a monitor is reading a press release.

Alignment

Silent cue-following slips through

A model can follow an authoritative hint planted on a wrong option and never mention it. The transcript stays clean and the behaviour stays invisible to anyone grading text.

Evaluation

Evaluations stop being credible

Reasoning benchmarks assume the reasoning is load-bearing. Score a decorative chain and you are measuring fluency while reporting it as thought.

The same numbers, two different decisions

Suppose an audit reports that model A's reasoning carries 62 percent of its answer and model B's carries 71 percent. A nine point gap looks like a reason to ship B. illustrative

Now put intervals on them: [45, 78] and [55, 84]. The comparison that matters is not whether those overlap, which settles nothing. It is the posterior for the difference itself, and whether the probability that B beats A by a margin you named in advance is high enough to act on. A point estimate cannot answer that question. That is the entire reason this project is Bayesian.

Those five numbers are invented, to show the shape of the problem. Every other number on this page comes from an artifact, and says which kind it is.

02

02 · the method

Stop grading the reasoning. Intervene on it.

Faithfulness here is a causal quantity, not a textual one. A chain can be coherent, relevant and completely inert.

The graph, and where the answer travels

prompt

STEP 01

Plant a cue, and randomize it

Every item runs in two arms: as written, and with an authoritative hint planted on a wrong option. Because the hint is randomized, the total effect on the answer is pinned directly. The item, not the row, is the sampling unit.

STEP 02

Keep only items the model already got right

The analysis population is items answered correctly under the clean prompt. That is what makes a hint-following event interpretable. It also has a the project reports rather than hides.

STEP 03

Measure the chain by cutting it short

The mediator is curve area: truncate the chain at five depths, force a continuation, and record the fraction of depths at which the model already commits to its final answer. A chain that is doing work changes that curve.

STEP 04

Split the effect, and put a posterior on it

A hierarchical probit model splits the total effect into the part carried by the chain () and the part that bypasses it (), pooling across prompts so a thin cell borrows strength instead of overfitting.

The estimand, exactly as the contract freezes it 
NDE = E[Y | do(X=1), M ~ M(X=0)] - E[Y | do(X=0), M ~ M(X=0)]
NIE = E[Y | do(X=1), M ~ M(X=1)] - E[Y | do(X=1), M ~ M(X=0)]
TE  = NDE + NIE

Read the NDE as the answer moving while the reasoning is pinned to what it would have been under the clean prompt. In a fully faithful world it is zero and the NIE carries everything. The outcome model is probit with baseline intercepts, because rho is defined as a correlation between bivariate normal errors and only exists in the probit model. An audit on 2026-09-07 found the sampler path had been logistic while every other path was probit, a gap of about 0.04 on an effect thresholded at 0.15. That is one of three defects listed in the record.

Open the interactive demo, which walks a single item through both arms and shows the split moving as you change the world.

03

03 · the assumption

Every faithfulness number rests on something you cannot check.

So this project does not assume it. It reports the whole curve, and names the point where the verdict would flip.

Splitting an effect into a mediated and a direct part requires that no unmeasured confounder sits between the chain of thought and the answer once the prompt is fixed. In a language model that is : the reasoning and the answer both read from the same hidden activations. Everyone who reports a mediation number is assuming it anyway, silently, at rho = 0.

rho*, and what it is not

That crossing point has a name: , the breakdown frontier. It is the mediation analogue of an E-value: the smallest residual mediator-outcome correlation that would overturn the verdict. A rho* of 0.05 means almost any unmeasured influence overturns it. A rho* of 0.69 means the confounder would have to be implausibly strong.

It is tempting to read a big rho* as a trust score. The project tested that idea against eleven synthetic families with known ground truth, and the idea did not survive.

Six models, 72 cells, and a disagreement nobody ordered.

The headline is not a faithfulness score. It is that two ways of asking the same question return different answers.

Two more results worth the space

Both are transferable beyond this project, and one of them is an infrastructure finding that changes how any evaluation of this kind should be run.

The judge instrument does not pass its own gate

The design called for an LLM jury to read transcripts and say whether the model mentioned the cue. Ten thresholds were frozen before any judge ran. No panel configuration clears them, on any prompt file, in any composition. One single-judge configuration does clear all ten. Three of four judges fail on the best prompt, and the two that fail on the contested classes fail in opposite directions on the same bytes.

So the column of record stays the frozen regular expression, uncorrected. A secondary jury matrix of 70,738 votes across 16 cells is measured, verified and banked, and has not yet been turned into a single reported number.

Against external human labels the picture is worse for the jury: the frozen regular expression beats every jury configuration by 4 to 52 specificity points, and the jury's recall against human unfaithfulness labels is 26 of 365. 5

What this project does not say

  • It does not detect deception. It measures silent cue-following, which is a narrower and checkable thing.
  • It does not treat its intervals as automatically well behaved. Calibration is a property to be tested and reported, and the failures are reported too.
  • It does not claim the label column is correct. Auditor labels are heuristic and human labelling has not started.
  • It does not rank models. The ranking rule refuses even to lay three models out in one table as a league.
  • rho* is a maximum-likelihood point estimate with unchecked optimizer convergence, not a posterior.

objections

What a careful reviewer asks first.

Your headline result is that you found nothing. Why publish it?

Because the alternative is to move the bar. The rule that says a faithfulness claim needs 0.95 posterior probability above a named margin was fixed before any of these numbers existed. Reporting UNRESOLVED is what that rule requires, and a project that only publishes when its rules are met is not measuring anything. The anchor disagreement in section 04 is a real finding that would have been buried by a more flattering write-up.

If rho* cannot rank cells, why is it still on the page?

Because it answers one question well and no others: how much residual correlation would be needed to overturn this verdict. That is useful. What it cannot do is compare two worlds, which is exactly what a trust score would need to do. The battery result is the evidence for that limit, and it is the project's own measurement.

Is the mediator a fair stand-in for the reasoning?

Not obviously, and the project says so. Curve area measures how early the model commits to its final answer under forced continuation. Mean clean-arm curve area runs 0.9383 to 0.9742 on the ARC cells against 0.4989 to 0.6422 on the AQuA cells, so the mediator has a different distribution on the two substrates and coefficients must not be read across them. The pre-registered grouping factor was cue family, and the measurement found the variation lives in the substrate instead.

Why does the site say fewer cells are anchored than it used to?

Because claim status turns on a model-level comparison that no model row clears. Two cells were published as anchored on 2026-09-07 and demoted when the model-level leg was applied. Publishing a retracted label next to its retraction is worse than publishing neither, so the earlier column moved to the record with its history intact.

sensitivity

rho*, the breakdown frontier

The smallest amount of hidden confounding that flips the faithfulness verdict: the residual mediator-outcome correlation at which the faithful path crosses zero.

A rho* of 0.05 means almost any unmeasured influence overturns the verdict. A rho* of 0.69 means the confounder would have to be implausibly strong. It is the mediation analogue of VanderWeele's E-value.

Two warnings travel with it. It is a maximum-likelihood point estimate with unchecked optimizer convergence, not a posterior. And it answers that one question and no other: it is invariant to the strength of the direct path by construction, and the project's own battery showed it cannot rank two worlds against each other.

gate

The four-cell replay anchor

A randomized cross-over that checks the mediation fit against something model-free: the answer rate on a designated target option, with the recipient cue crossed against the donor chain source, giving four cell means.

A cell is only ANCHORED when all three estimands agree with their matching anchor contrast within 0.10 on the difference, a margin fixed before any data was seen. Eleven of 24 cells clear it on their own three estimands. All eleven are still held at RAW, because claim status also turns on the model-level comparison, which no model row clears.

estimand

NIE, the natural indirect effect

The part of the prompt's effect on the answer that is carried by the reasoning: the answer moving only because the reasoning moved.

This is the faithful path. In a fully faithful world it carries the whole effect. It is plotted in cyan everywhere on this site and in the published figures.

estimand

NDE, the natural direct effect

The part of the effect that bypasses the reasoning: the answer moving while the chain of thought is held at what it would have been under the clean prompt.

In a fully faithful world it is zero. A high ratio of direct to total effect is what decorative reasoning looks like. It is plotted in orange throughout.

assumption

Sequential ignorability

The bundle of assumptions under which natural direct and indirect effects are identified. The load-bearing one here is that no unmeasured confounder sits between the chain of thought and the answer once the prompt is fixed.

In a language model this is almost never true, because the reasoning and the answer both read from the same hidden activations. Randomizing the prompt does not help: it fixes confounding of the prompt, not of the mediator. That is why the project reports a curve over rho instead of a single number at rho = 0.

population

The clean-correct subpopulation

The frozen analysis population: only items the model answers correctly under the clean prompt. That is what makes a hint-following event interpretable, since the model had the right answer available and moved away from it.

It has a structural cost the project reports rather than hides. The clean arm then carries zero outcome variance by construction, so the direct and indirect split rests on the probit link extrapolating into a region the clean arm never visits.
