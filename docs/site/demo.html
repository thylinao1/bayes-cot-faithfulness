What am I looking at?

Three plain ideas, and the formal names underneath in case you want them.

The faithful path

Particles that travel up through the reasoning node and down to the answer. The thicker and busier this path, the more the model's stated reasoning is genuinely causing its answer.

prompt → reasoning → answer

The shortcut

Particles that skip straight across the bottom. This is the answer arriving without the reasoning, a sign the written steps are decorative rather than load-bearing.

prompt → answer (bypasses reasoning)

The score

Faithfulness is the share of the answer's movement that went through the reasoning. The real method puts a calibrated uncertainty band on this number and asks how much hidden confounding it would take to overturn it.
